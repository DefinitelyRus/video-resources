# Why young people are leaving the church behind

## Intro

On one hand, most young people would probably agree that spirituality itself is neither inherently good or bad.

It sets what they consider acceptable behavior---their moral compass, which---perhaps obviously---is a good thing to have.



On the other hand, young people are increasingly becoming non-religious.

They would often display resentment or apathy when pushed to partake in religious activities.



In just X years, there's been a X% decline in Christian participation among Gen-Z. That's Y% or Z million people of the entire Gen-Z population in the US alone.

So, if spirituality sets their moral compass, and Z million of them chose to leave their religion and therefore their moral compass along with it, how come there's no anarchy brewing up in a corner somewhere?



If that sounds like a ridiculous question to ask, that's because it is.

Gen-Z has *not*---in fact---lost all morality, perhaps they just found a new moral compass.
